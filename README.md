# Eva by WearMeds

Team Sanjo-X · NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Rosario · Challenge: *Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions*.

Eva is the Super Intelligence health copilot inside the WearMeds wristband, for passengers on a commercial trip in space. Eva **guides, records and warns**: when someone cannot act, it guides the nearest passenger; the ship's emergency station lights the right numbered compartment (configurable for each ship); it warns of solar particle events using NASA DONKI and NOAA GOES data; and it records every step. The ship hub hosts the Super Intelligence and stores everything; Earth receives and acknowledges the signed records. Eva gives diagnostic support only: the AED decides the shock.
