AI-generated images. Every illustration here is AI-generated concept art for team Sanjo-X (NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026, Rosario): the 3D icons, Eva's mascot states, the app icon, the launch screen, the large detail photos and the wristband scenes. Prompts were written by the team with Claude; the images were generated with an OpenAI image model through Codex.

Where it says so. The launch screen and the large photos carry their own "AI-generated" mark. This notice, and the line at the foot of each screen, cover the small icons and Eva's mascot states.

No agency branding. The images are illustrative and contain no NASA insignia or branding.

What Eva does. Eva gives diagnostic support: she reads the wristband, records, guides and warns. The crew lead confirms, and the ship's AED decides shocks.
