# Style: "NASA Horizon × luminous editorial"

A mashup of NASA's Horizon Design System (clean, engineered, trustworthy) and a warm luminous editorial look (deep plum/wine/forest stages, soft blurred light, floating white objects).

## Palette (use these hues; nothing neon)
- Deep plum #281647 · Wine #421d24 · Forest #0c4243 (stage backgrounds)
- Warm paper #f7f5f2 · Lavender #e8e0ff · Lilac #d4c7ff (light surfaces, glows)
- Signal red #f64137 (only tiny alert lights) · Signal blue #1c67e3 / #288bff (interaction glows) · Amber #ffcb77 (the "illuminated item" glow) · Active green #47da84 (tiny "OK" lights)
- Carbon neutrals #e3e3e3 / #58585b / #1d1d1d for hardware

## Light and materials
- Soft, cinematic, slightly diffused. Key light warm from upper left, cool lilac rim light from the right.
- Background glows: large out-of-focus violet and lilac light blooms (40–80px blur feel), like light through frosted glass.
- Hardware: matte white and soft-grey polymer, rounded corners, subtle chamfers, small recessed status LEDs. Believable 2030s consumer-medical design (think premium fitness wearable, not sci-fi armor).
- Spacecraft interiors: commercial-cabin comfort (soft upholstery, warm indirect light, rounded windows), not military.

## Composition
- Scenes leave clear negative space (left third) for headlines.
- Layers are centered, isolated, transparent background, full object visible with ~8% padding, soft contact shadow optional (separate from object is fine).
- Camera: 3/4 front, eye level, 50mm feel. No fisheye.

## Never
Logos, text, numbers, flags, patches, recognizable faces, neon cyberpunk, heavy lens flares, clutter.
