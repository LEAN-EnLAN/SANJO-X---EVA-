# AI-generated content notice

All images in this archive were generated with OpenAI GPT Image 2.5 (gpt-image-2.5-sunburst for finals, gpt-image-2.5-flare for drafts) and orchestrated by Codex (gpt-6.1-terra), from prompts written by team Sanjo-X with Claude. Some were generated as edits of earlier AI-generated images from kit v1 (gpt-image-2). The exact model that ran each image is recorded in manifest.json.

Post-processing was done locally with Python/Pillow: edges blended into pure black or white, a visible watermark stamped on scenes, resizing, and contact shadows drawn from each layer's alpha. Shadows and chips were made locally without any AI model.

The images are illustrative concept art. They are not photographs or NASA imagery, they contain no NASA insignia or branding, and they depict no real people.

Per NASA Space Apps rules, AI-generated images and videos must carry a visible AI watermark when published, and AI use must be declared in the project description.
